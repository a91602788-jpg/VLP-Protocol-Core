# Research source register

## Scope and review meaning

This register records primary references for the initial VLP research questions. **Entry review date: 2026-10-19.** That date applies to every entry below and means a bibliographic and targeted content review, including identification of relevant sections. It does not mean a complete audit of the publication, its errata, an implementation, or a proposed VLP design.

All entries are research references. No transport, handshake, cipher suite, carrier, camouflage mechanism, or performance claim is approved by inclusion here. Decisions belong in the [decision process](../process/decision-process.md) and must be traceable to [requirements](../requirements/README.md).

The register distinguishes stable publication identifiers from changing project documentation. Version numbers mentioned in a documentation page are not evidence of the latest released implementation. Release and commit pinning remain necessary before implementation comparisons or experiments.

## Gaps to close before a Research Gate decision

- Map each proposed requirement and novelty claim to relevant primary references, counterexamples, and unresolved assumptions. This is an initial register, not an exhaustive prior-art search.
- Check relevant RFC errata, updates, and related standards at the point of adopting a mechanism. No complete errata review has been recorded here.
- Resolve carrier-specific interaction with the refreshed TLS and hybrid references, and recheck multipath publication and implementation support before selecting a profile.
- Resolve the FIPS 203 planning note and evaluate any proposed hybrid composition separately from the primitive standard.
- Pin the source code, releases, build configuration, platform, and enabled modes of every comparison implementation. Review whether documentation and implementation agree.
- Review complete paper methods and artifacts before relying on experimental conclusions or reproducing a benchmark.
- Define the observation and attacker models for traffic-analysis claims. Treat protocol correctness, payload confidentiality, metadata exposure, resource cost, and detection measurements as separate questions.
- Check the terms and attribution requirements before copying source material. This register links and summarizes references; it does not import their contents or grant reuse rights. See [Licensing](../LICENSING.md).

## Maintaining entries

Keep source identifiers stable when updating an entry. For a changed publication or web page, record the new review date, document revision or commit where available, changed claims, and affected requirements or decisions. If a claim cannot be checked, record the limitation rather than replacing it with an unsupported conclusion.

New entries should include the primary URL, publication identity or revision, exact relevant sections, the date and scope of review, the research question they inform, and outstanding checks. A source review and a project approval remain separate records.
